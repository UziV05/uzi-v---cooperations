Meet the Founder & CEO

Anonye Victor Izuchukwu

A passionate innovator, data enthusiast, and technology-driven thinker based in Federal Housing Authority, Lugbe, Abuja. With a B.Sc in Statistics and a Diploma in AI, Graphics Designs, and Web Development. Devoted to transforming lives and shaping the world through ICT, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science.

His vision goes beyond Nigeria — committed to expanding technology’s reach to Africa and the world, advancing human life and wellbeing through innovation, creativity, and purposeful digital transformation.

Great gratitude also extends to his siblings Miss Confidence and Miss Ifeoma Anonye for their constructive criticism and support, for shaping this brand into what it has become. To friends, The team, and our dedicated staff — your faith and effort keeps this vision alive.

Inspired daily by God’s guidance to pursue consistency, resilience, and hard work. He's purpose is to create impact through technology that enhances lives and fosters progress.
